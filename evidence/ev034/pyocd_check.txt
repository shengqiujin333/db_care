Conclusion
- Probe is present and the generic Cortex-M0+ core is discovered.
- No CW32L010 DFP target is installed; generic `cortex_m` is the only usable target.
- HW reset (nRESET) through the CW-DAPLink works and is the method that produced the
  BOT-01 capture (see capture_with_reset.py / bot01_hw.bin).
- pyocd is NOT registered as an entry in runtime/environment.yaml.  It was used only
  because the Project Goal's supplementary condition #2 explicitly said to try the
  connected CW-DAPLink and pyocd, and because the reset is required to capture the
  boot lines.  Nothing was installed and no configuration was changed.
  Recommendation to the configuration owner: register a pyocd reset entry (target
  cortex_m, probe unique ID, timeout) or load WHXY.CW32L010_DFP 1.0.2.
